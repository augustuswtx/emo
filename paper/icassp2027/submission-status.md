# ICASSP 2027 submission status

Updated: 2026-08-11

## Venue decision (superseded as primary route)

ICASSP 2027 remains a technically valid CCF-B backup because the paper studies acoustic, visual, and textual reliability and the official scope includes Multimedia Signal Processing, Machine Learning for Signal Processing, and Speech and Language Processing. It is not the primary recommendation after accounting for the absence of deadline pressure: the four-page technical-content limit removes too much of the reliability audit, negative evidence, and cross-dataset analysis.

IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing is now the working primary target. The present evidence is not yet journal-complete: MOSEI, realistic corruptions, final-schedule actionability controls, and efficiency measurements are unfinished. ICME remains a reasonable conference fallback, but its 2027 call and paper kit were not yet available for a current-policy submission plan.
