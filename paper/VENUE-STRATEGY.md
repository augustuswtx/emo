# Venue strategy

Updated: 2026-08-11

## Primary target: IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing

IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing (TAC) is the working primary target. The current CCF artificial-intelligence directory lists TAC as a B-tier journal. Its scope directly covers systems that recognize, interpret, and model human emotion and related affective phenomena, matching this project's multimodal sentiment, modality-reliability, and evaluation focus.

This choice prioritizes thematic fit and a complete evidence package over a nearby conference deadline. The manuscript should remain in its long-form evidence-grounded structure until the following gates are resolved:

1. Complete the frozen MOSEI comparison without changing P4 based on test outcomes.
2. Add realistic acoustic and visual corruptions beyond Gaussian feature noise.
3. Repeat the most important constant, permuted, reversed, and oracle controls under the final schedule, or narrow the actionability claim.
4. Add efficiency reporting: parameter count, training time, inference time, and memory.
5. Explain the allocation direction through gradient or sample-difficulty analysis.
6. Recheck the audio-length confound on MOSEI.
7. Add a full method figure, corruption protocol figure, and claim-aligned ablation tables.

The intended journal story is not “a new image model.” It is: quality-aware affective computing needs auditable intermediate reliability; fixed-budget auxiliary allocation isolates whether sample assignment matters without changing total supervision.

## Conference backups

- ICME: a more natural multimedia-conference backup than a vision-only venue, but the 2027 official call and template should be checked when available.
- ICASSP: technically in scope through multimedia signal processing and currently CCF-B, but its four-page technical-content limit removes too much of the audit and limitation evidence. Retain the existing compact draft only as a backup.
- ICMR: CCF-B but less suitable because the paper is not primarily a multimedia retrieval contribution.

## Journal-format policy

Use the standard IEEE Transactions journal template as a working format only after the MOSEI evidence is integrated. Before submission, recheck TAC-specific article types, page limits, overlength charges, supplementary-material policy, AI-use disclosure, and ScholarOne requirements against current official sources.
